# Optional four-energy teaching comparison

The main workflow is H1 followed by a continuous effective excitation energy
distribution. The four spectroscopic 4s energies are motivation and a comparison
after fitting. They are not fixed components in the continuous inference.

The saved tables under `reference/optional_h4s/` belong to the earlier specified
four-energy periodic-response model, with leave-one-retarding-curve-out fits.
They retain their historical five-curve scope. They are supplied for reading
and classroom comparison, and are separate from the current convolution model.
The complete earlier model-development records are retained in the private
research archive. Current menu mode 3 does not rerun these historical fits.

Students identify the reference separations prescribed by spectroscopy, then
identify fitted common shifts and response weights. A separate convolution
exercise combines the four discrete energies in successive collisions. Compare
that prescribed representation with the effective distribution in the main
worked example. The historical tables contain their original diagnostic fields;
those fields are not a model ranking for the current article.
